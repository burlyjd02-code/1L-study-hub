Foundational Quick Reference (Reasoning, Rules, Authority, Research & Guided Memo Prompts):
• Rule Structure: Elements (all required; disjunctive "or" vs. conjunctive "and"), Factors (weighed together, no single factor required), Balancing (weighing competing interests), Totality of Circumstances.
• Legal Reasoning: Rule-based (deductive rule + facts), Analogical (fact-to-fact similarity/contrast + court's reasoning), Policy-based (purpose, judicial administration, fairness).
• Authority & Citations: Primary vs. secondary; binding vs. persuasive; citations at end of rule/E sentences (zero citations in A for course memos); "will likely" predictions.
• Guided Memo Prompts: (1) Closed-packet statute/case synthesis ("smoothie, not parfait"); (2) Sub-rule E paragraphs with full FHR; (3) Parallel A thesis (no case names) + "Although" counterpoint resolution.
